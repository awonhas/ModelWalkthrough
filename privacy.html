Privacy Policy

Model Walkthrough for Apple Vision Pro

Support Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The short version

Model Walkthrough does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything the app does happens on your Apple Vision Pro.

Your models

When you open a USDZ file, the app makes a temporary copy on your device so it can display it. Your models are never uploaded or sent anywhere, and the temporary copy is removed when you open another model or the system clears temporary files.

Head position and movement

During a walkthrough, the app uses visionOS to read where your head is and which way you're facing, so it can move you through the model and keep the ground beneath your feet. This information is used only while the app is running, is processed entirely on your device, and is never stored or shared.

No accounts, analytics or advertising

The app has no sign-in, contains no advertising, and includes no analytics or tracking tools. It does not use your data to track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
